## Product redlines

- Keep four facts separate: deterministic Quant output, DeepSeek advice, subsequent real market outcome, and user-confirmed real execution.
- Candidate plans, triggers, Advisor responses, and shadow scores never write the real Ledger. Only a user-confirmed actual fill, explicit cash reconciliation, or audited Ledger correction may change it. Never create simulated cash, positions, fills, or returns.
- Do not automatically place orders. Plans are for user review; stale or unreliable data, missing evidence, failed guards, or an unqualified strategy must wait or show `NO_TRADE`.
- Do not claim a probability, prediction, win rate, or strategy qualification without a defined metric, sufficient evidence, and appropriate out-of-sample validation. Regime confidence is not a price-direction probability.
- Never invent or interpolate quotes, minute bars, IOPV, breadth, fills, costs, user preferences, market mappings, or NAS research. Preserve source, market time, capture time, freshness, and explicit unavailable reasons.
- Keep NAS on the historical research/archive path. Advisor may use an already-cached, still-fresh Research Summary, but it must not synchronously wait for NAS. A missing summary must not stop base Advisor analysis. NAS is not a prerequisite for live iPhone quotes.
- NAS history is primarily for Quant research, backtest, validation, out-of-sample evaluation, strategy qualification/versioning, and later distribution to iPhone Local Quant. DeepSeek Research Summary is an optional secondary use.
- Market Regime is a deterministic multi-factor Quant input. Index state must not be assigned to an ETF without an explicit, verified relationship. Missing country/market coverage remains unavailable; do not use A-share state as a substitute for Nasdaq, Hong Kong, Japan, or Korea.
- Keep changes local until the user changes the current no-commit/no-push instruction.
- Limit GitHub Actions to at most two running workflows at once. Keep one concurrency group per repository workflow and do not cancel a running build.
